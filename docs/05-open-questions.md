# Open Questions

## Purpose

These decisions are intentionally unresolved. They should be tested or discussed rather than silently invented during implementation.

## Product identity

- Product name and verbal identity.
- Exact initial audience language beyond culturally engaged, aesthetically aware book-and-film users.
- Whether public profiles may be fully private or followers-only in the alpha.

## Thoughts

- Exact maximum size of the compact statement and optional body.
- Whether correction history is public for every edit or only edits after a short grace period.
- Withdrawal and deletion behaviour after a Thought has Comments, references, or Appreciations.
- Whether a Thought may use more than one precise scene or passage reference per Media anchor.

## Map

- Which automatic layout best balances stability, legibility, and visual character.
- Which light controls ship first: pinning, featuring regions, manual Theme membership, or all three.
- How Drafts appear in the private Map without being confused with Published Thoughts.
- The threshold at which large Maps need time, Theme, or density views.

## Themes

- How cluster suggestions are generated without making AI the author of identity.
- How similar personal Themes are matched for public Theme spaces.
- How a User understands and corrects an inaccurate similarity suggestion.

## Social system

- Whether Appreciations notify immediately or appear in a quiet activity digest.
- Whether Following and follower lists are both public while counts remain hidden.
- Exact Comment moderation and deletion rules.
- Whether authors may disable cross-person references independently from Comments.
- How substantial corrections to a referenced Thought are surfaced to connected authors.

## Media catalogue

- Initial metadata providers for Books and Films.
- Provisional-record verification and duplicate-merging workflow.
- Artwork licensing, caching, provenance, and correction policy.
- Whether editions and alternate film cuts remain metadata or eventually become separate records.

## Scope

- Whether viewing seeded Paths belongs in Stage 0 even though authoring is deferred.
- Whether a basic activity surface is necessary in the prototype for Appreciations and Comments.
- Which analytics are ethically appropriate and useful for evaluating the core loop.

## Decision rule

When resolving an open question, prefer the option that:

1. Strengthens the User's sense of identity and authorship.
2. Keeps the Map meaningful rather than decorative.
3. Encourages human interpretation over passive consumption.
4. Preserves clear credit and context.
5. Avoids rebuilding ratings, feeds, forums, or productivity software.
6. Can be tested with the smallest honest implementation.
