# Open questions

## Purpose

These decisions remain intentionally unresolved. They are owner boundaries to
test or discuss, not an implementation backlog. Settled foundations belong in
the Product Foundation and must not be reopened silently during implementation.

## Product identity and audience

- Final verbal identity, tagline, and public explanation of the name Contour.
- Exact initial audience language for culturally curious, visually expressive
  Book-and-Film Users.
- Whether launch messaging should emphasize Books, Films, or cross-media
  discovery while the product continues to support both.
- Whether public profiles and Maps may be fully private or followers-only in a
  later alpha.

## Discovery and recommendations

- Which candidate-generation approach supplies honest value in the first real
  multi-User test.
- Which human evidence should be required before a recommendation is presented
  confidently rather than as an inferred possibility.
- How direct Thoughts, connections, personal Themes, similar Maps, follows,
  Saves, Bookmarks, Votes, searches, and passive visits should be weighted.
- How Contour preserves serendipity and opposing perspectives without making
  personalization feel random.
- Which explanation language is understandable without revealing another
  User's private behavior.
- How a User corrects, resets, exports, or disables their private interest
  history.
- Appropriate retention periods and consent boundaries for passive behavioral
  signals.
- How recommendation quality can be evaluated without using time spent or
  daily activity as a proxy.

## Likes, Saves, Bookmarks, and Votes

- Exact public Like presentation on Media pages, profiles, and Maps.
- Whether a Like requires an explicit statement that it will appear on the
  public Map.
- Whether a work remains in the Map after its Like is removed when it still has
  a Draft but no Published Thought.
- Exact upvote and downvote presentation and how strongly one Vote should alter
  recommendations.
- Whether authors receive a quiet positive notice when their Thought helps
  someone, without a public count.
- Whether Bookmarking a public Theme region stores a live route or a private
  snapshot.

## Thoughts

- Exact maximum size of the compact statement and optional body.
- Whether correction history is public for every edit or only edits after a
  short grace period.
- Withdrawal and deletion behavior after a Thought has Comments, references,
  Bookmarks, or discovery provenance.
- Whether a Thought may use more than one precise scene or passage reference
  per Media anchor.
- Whether any lightweight creation prompt improves specificity without
  constraining free expression.

## Map

- Which automatic layout best balances stability, legibility, and visual
  character as taste-only Media and authored Thoughts grow together.
- How the starter Map distinguishes a public Like from a Published Thought at
  every semantic-zoom level.
- The threshold at which large Maps need time, Theme, density, or authored-only
  views.
- Whether aesthetic customization should ever extend beyond moving, pinning,
  connecting, featuring, and Theme visibility.
- Whether and how someone may add a live, copied, or external branch of another
  person's Map while preserving authorship, provenance, edits, and the
  distinction between reference and agreement.

The current safe branch behavior is Bookmark, navigate, and publish a credited
response. No import behavior is authorized.

## Personal Themes

- The evidence threshold at which a Map has enough substance and coherence to
  generate a Theme.
- How generated names communicate provenance without looking provisional or
  generic.
- How Theme generation balances Likes, authored Thoughts, explicit
  connections, and recurring language.
- How similar personal Themes are matched without merging them into a canonical
  global topic.
- How a User understands, corrects, hides, dismisses, and later restores an
  inaccurate Theme.
- Whether a hidden Theme remains eligible for the owner's private
  personalization while remaining absent from public discovery.

## Social system and safety

- Whether Comments improve human connection enough to enter the first shared
  phase.
- Exact Comment moderation, notification, and deletion rules.
- Whether Following and follower lists are visible while counts remain hidden.
- Whether authors may disable cross-person references independently from
  Comments.
- How substantial corrections to a referenced Thought are surfaced to
  connected authors and discovery results.
- How blocking affects existing references, Bookmarks, and recommendation
  explanations.

## Media catalogue

- Initial metadata providers for Books and Films.
- Provisional-record verification and duplicate-merging workflow.
- Artwork licensing, caching, provenance, and correction policy.
- Whether editions and alternate film cuts remain metadata or eventually
  become separate records.

## Scope and validation

- The exact owner-approved goal for the proposed Human Discovery Prototype.
- Which seeded or forced recommendation behavior is honest enough for that
  prototype.
- Whether viewing seeded Paths belongs in a later prototype.
- Which analytics are ethically appropriate and useful for evaluating natural
  discovery and contribution moments.
- What target-user evidence is sufficient to progress from a forced discovery
  prototype to a closed multi-User alpha.

## Decision rule

When resolving an open question, prefer the option that:

1. Improves the chance of finding a worthwhile unfamiliar Book or Film.
2. Makes the human reason behind discovery understandable.
3. Preserves deliberate authorship, provenance, and public/private boundaries.
4. Keeps the Map useful and legible as taste and expression accumulate.
5. Allows varied interpretation without global truth or popularity scores.
6. Avoids attention-maximizing feeds, streaks, and artificial urgency.
7. Can be tested through the smallest honest end-to-end behavior.
